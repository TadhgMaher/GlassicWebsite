Built-in calculators

Three footprints, measured together

AI's impact isn't just carbon. We measure all three so nothing is hidden.

Carbon

Electricity used to train and run models, converted using the carbon intensity of the grids that power the data centres.

Water

Data centres drink water for cooling. We estimate on-site and indirect water use per request, by region.

Emissions

Wider greenhouse-gas emissions, including embodied hardware impact, expressed in tonnes of CO₂ equivalent.

    Methodology & trust

    Measured honestly, not marketed

    AI footprint science is young. We'd rather be clear about what we know than claim false precision.

    How we estimate

    We combine model and hardware energy data, data-centre efficiency, regional grid carbon intensity and cooling water use. Where providers publish figures, we use them; otherwise we use published research ranges and say so.

    Uncertainty and conservative rounding

    Every figure carries a confidence range. When we are unsure we round up, so you buy slightly more offset rather than less.

    Credit quality

    We only fund projects verified by recognised standards, preferring permanent and additional outcomes, and publish the registry reference for each.

    Independent audit

    Our methodology and ledger are reviewed annually by a third party, with results published here.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Do we need to change how our teams use AI?

    No. Glassic measures usage alongside your existing tools; the aim is that nobody has to change their workflow.

    Is carbon offsetting enough?

    Offsets are not a licence to waste. That is why the dashboard highlights where usage can be reduced first, and offsetting covers what remains.

    How accurate are the numbers?

    As accurate as available data allows, always shown with a range and a method note. See the methodology section above.

    Can we really see where our money went?

    Yes. Each month's investment is matched to named projects in a public ledger.
